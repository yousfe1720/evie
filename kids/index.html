CAL Kids

A safer screen. A friendly guide.

CAL Kids is designed for children and teens up to 14: it watches over the screen, answers their curious questions in a voice made for them, and keeps parents in the loop without invading their space.

What it is designed to do

Protection that feels like help.

  • Active Screen ShieldScreen Awareness watches the screen live and closes any page or window at once if violent, dangerous or unsuitable content appears.
  • Instant Parent AlertsBlocks quickly when harmful content is reached, then sends parents a notification with what happened.
  • Hover & LearnHover over any picture or complex text and ask “Hey CAL, what is this?” for a simple, friendly spoken explanation.
  • Smart Time ManagementSeparates play time from school tasks and gently lets your child know when entertainment time is over.
  • Parents DashboardA weekly report on your child’s interests and talents, based on their questions, plus safety and blocked-content reports.
  • Zero Data LoggingScreen content is filtered in a way designed so that your child’s data, voice and interests are not stored or sent to outside servers.
