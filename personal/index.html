Your everyday secretary and companion.

Email, files, appointments, research, translation: CAL Personal is designed to take the small, constant work of daily life off your plate, and remember what matters to you.

What it is designed to do

Everyday help, in one place.

  • Email and messagesPrioritize, summarize and draft replies for your review.
  • Files and personal informationOrganize and find what you need.
  • ResearchApartments, services, destinations, shopping options.
  • Appointments and travelHelp arrange them, with your approval.
  • Schedules and meetingsCoordinate your calendar and prepare notes.
  • Calls to clinics and providersHelp prepare and, in future, support the conversation.
  • Language and translationLearn, translate and understand.
  • RemindersGentle nudges for the things that matter.
  • Personal contextUseful memory across interactions, under your control.
  • Multiple devicesWork across the supported devices you choose.
