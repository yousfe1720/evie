Your AI executive assistant for work.

For founders, professionals and teams: one assistant that prepares the meeting, coordinates the follow-up, and keeps projects moving across the tools you already use.

Connected workspaces

Communications, documents and tasks, in step.

CAL is designed to sit across your workspaces and hand work between them, so the preparation is done before the meeting starts.

“Prepare me for tomorrow’s investor meeting.”

  1. Reads the calendar and attendeesWho is in the room and what changed.
    Calendar
  2. Gathers notes and past follow-upsWhat was promised, what is open.
    Documents
  3. Researches the attendees’ recent newsSummarized with references to check.
    Research
  4. Coordinates with other AI agentsHands work to specialists, collects results.
    Agents
  5. Queues follow-ups for your reviewNothing is sent until you say so.
    Review

What it is designed to do

Work, coordinated.

  • Email and calendar coordinationScheduling, triage and drafts.
  • Meeting preparation and follow-upBriefs before, actions after.
  • Research and reportingStructured, sourced summaries.
  • Marketing workflowsContent and campaign organization.
  • Coordination with other AI agentsCAL as the point of contact.
  • Coding assistanceDevelopment workflows alongside your tools.
  • Project organizationTasks, owners, deadlines in one view.
  • Business planningFrameworks and drafts to react to.
  • Document preparation and reviewFirst drafts and careful review.
  • Sales and customer communicationDrafts and follow-ups for your approval.
  • Workflow automationThrough supported tools.

CAL assists with documents, analysis and preparation. It does not provide legal or financial advice, and it is not designed to make autonomous legal or financial decisions on your behalf.
