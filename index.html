    Your Intelligence. Your Context. Your Control.

    A dedicated personal environment designed to keep your context, preferences and connected workflows under your control. These are design goals, described honestly on the privacy page.

    • Personal data isolationYour context kept apart from everyone else’s and from public information.
    • Permission-based accessTools and devices connect only when you allow it, and you can see and change what is connected.
    • Clear controlsTransparent retention and deletion policies, and encryption in transit and at rest where implemented.
    How Personal Cloud is designed

    Technology that meets people where they are.

    Voice instead of tiny buttons. Plain language instead of jargon. Translation in the moment you need it.

    Deaf, hard-of-hearing and non-speaking people

    CAL understands everyone, including Deaf and non-speaking people.

    CAL understands English sign language. Connect it to your camera and talk to it by signing. And when you can’t hear, CAL talks to you in sign language, so a call, a clinic visit or a school meeting doesn’t depend on hearing or speaking.

    • Understands English sign languageConnect CAL to your camera and talk to it by signing.
    • Talks back in sign languageWhen you can’t hear, CAL speaks to you in sign language.
    • Live captionsCalls, meetings and conversations written out as they happen.
    • Speaks aloud for youCAL says your words out loud on calls and at the counter.
