How usage works

No tokens and no credits. Here is what is included, what has a limit, and what other companies bill.

Included

Everyday chat, reminders, files, simple tasks and background tasks. Covered by fair use, so you never count messages.

Has a stated limit

Devices, call minutes, storage and app events a day. Each is a number you can see before you buy. At a limit, tasks pause with a clear message and nothing is charged without your approval.

Billed by others

Phone calls beyond your included minutes, phone numbers, SMS and outside AI services. You see the cost in your call balance. Emergency calls from Care are never limited.

Questions

Is CAL available now?

Not yet. CAL is in its waitlist phase and joining the waitlist is the only action available today. Prices here are launch estimates and may change. Nothing is billed.

What does “no tokens to run out” mean?

CAL does not charge by tokens or credits. You pay one flat price for your plan. The only limits are plain ones: devices, call minutes, storage and app events a day.

What does the Free plan include?

Free gives you a limited set of everyday features on one device: chat with a daily limit, a few web searches a day, reminders, notes, and all accessibility features. It does not include WhatsApp, phone calls or always-on tasks.

Are phone calls included?

Executive includes 2 hours a month and Super includes 3 hours. Beyond that, calls are billed by the call provider from a balance you can see. Personal does not include phone calls.

Can I add Care later?

Yes. Add CAL Care to Personal or Executive at any time, take only the parts you need, and choose how many people you care for. It is included in Super for 2 care profiles.

Is CAL Care a medical service?

No. CAL can alert your contacts based on the devices you connect. It is not a medical device and not a replacement for emergency services.

Do you offer yearly billing?

Yes, at 20% off. Use the Yearly switch at the top of this page.

What happens when I press “Get” on a plan?

You go to a short page where you can review the plan, add CAL Care if you want it, and add yourself to the waitlist with your choices. Nothing is billed.

What does “Contact Sales” do?

It opens our contact page so we can scope Enterprise needs with you.
