Questions

Short answers before you post.

Which platforms count?

Any public one: X, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, Threads, a blog or a newsletter. If people can open it without an account, it counts.

How much will my post earn?

Reach counts most, but a post that teaches something well can earn a reward with modest numbers. The team decides the amount when we review it.

Do the discounts stack with the yearly price?

The reward is worked out when we review your post and applies to the plan you choose at launch. We will confirm the exact price in the email we send you.

How do I get the credits and the discount?

We email you at the address you give us, within a week of reviewing, to arrange your Claude credits and your plan discount.

Can I send a post I made before this page existed?

Yes, as long as it is still public. Older posts are reviewed the same way.

Is this a paid review?

No. Nobody is paid before posting, and we never ask for a particular opinion. Rewards go to posts that have already reached people, whatever they concluded.

Who sees what I send?

Your link, name, email, referral code and note are stored in a private sheet that only the CAL team can open, the same one we use for the waitlist. We use your email only to reply to you.
